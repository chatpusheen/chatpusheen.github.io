Privacy policy

The Chat Pusheen tool is an internal application. Its only user is the Google account belonging to the law office that operates it. It uses Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Gmail, under that one account, to create and organize documents, record review comments, and read and answer mail sent to that account.

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about anyone other than what already resides in the office's own Google account. Data accessed through Google APIs is used only to provide the tool's functions for that account and is not transferred to any third party, used for advertising, or used to train models. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings, which ends all access immediately.

Questions about this policy can be sent to chatpusheenlaw@gmail.com.
